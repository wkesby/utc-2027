UTC 2027 is now an app

Live standings, every drafter's stats and the full draft board — on your phone, updated daily.

Open the app →

Put it on your home screen

iPhone (Safari)

  1. Open this link in Safari (not Chrome — only Safari can install).
  2. Tap the Share button ⤴ at the bottom.
  3. Scroll down, tap Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Android (Chrome)

  1. Open this link in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu, top right.
  3. Tap Install app (or Add to Home screen), then confirm.

What you get

  • ◆Ladder — live totals, confirmed vs in-play points, and who moved overnight.
  • ◆Stats — steal & bust of the season, points above par, comp wins, and whether drafting early actually helped.
  • ◆Comps — all 20 competitions with the full draft board and live positions.
  • ◆Drafters — tap anyone for their form guide, all 20 picks and head-to-head record against you.

Not seeing the latest version?

Close the app completely — swipe it away from the app switcher — then open it again, twice if needed. Or open this page in your browser and pull down to refresh. Updates arrive on their own after that.

Good to know

It refreshes itself every day at 4am, works offline on the train, and costs nothing. No account, no login, no app store. If your picks are going badly, that is not a bug.

Trouble installing? Just bookmark wkesby.github.io/utc-2027 — it works fine in any browser.
